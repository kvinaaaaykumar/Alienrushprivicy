Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. Learn how ALIEN RUSH handles information when you enjoy our mobile game.

Last Updated: November 8, 2026

Welcome to ALIEN RUSH

This Privacy Policy applies to the ALIEN RUSH mobile game developed by Sumit Sharma. It explains what information may be collected through the game, how third-party services may use that information, and how you can contact us with privacy questions.

01

Introduction

Welcome to ALIEN RUSH, a mobile arcade survival game. We respect your privacy and are committed to transparency about information associated with your use of the game.

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully to understand our privacy practices.

02

Information We Collect

ALIEN RUSH does not require users to create an account or provide their name, phone number, or email address to play.

Third-party services integrated into the game may automatically collect certain information, depending on the services and device settings:

  • Device information and device identifiers.
  • IP address.
  • Advertising identifiers.
  • Advertising interactions.
  • Game usage and interaction information.
  • Crash reports and diagnostic information.
03

Advertising & Google AdMob

ALIEN RUSH may display advertisements through Google AdMob, including rewarded and interstitial advertisements.

Google AdMob and its partners may process information such as device identifiers, IP addresses, and advertising interactions for advertisement delivery, performance measurement, and fraud prevention.

Learn more about Google's privacy practices: Google Privacy Policy .

04

Third-Party Services

Our game may use the following third-party services:

  • Google AdMob: Advertising services.
  • Google Play Services: Game distribution and related platform services.

These services may process information according to their own privacy policies and practices.

05

Children's Privacy

ALIEN RUSH is intended for users aged 13 years and older. The game is not specifically designed or marketed for children under 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13. If we become aware that personal information has been collected directly from a child without appropriate authorization, we will take reasonable steps to address the situation.

Advertising and data collection are subject to applicable privacy laws and Google Play policies.

06

How We Use Information

Information processed through third-party services may be used for purposes such as:

  • Displaying advertisements.
  • Improving game stability and performance.
  • Detecting technical problems.
  • Preventing fraud and abuse.
  • Measuring advertising effectiveness.

We do not sell personal information collected directly by us.

07

Data Sharing

ALIEN RUSH does not operate its own user-account registration system.

Information may be processed by third-party service providers, including Google AdMob and Google Play Services, according to their respective privacy policies.

08

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information associated with ALIEN RUSH. However, no method of electronic storage or internet transmission is completely secure.

09

Data Retention & Deletion

Information collected by third-party services is retained according to their respective privacy policies and retention practices.

For privacy-related questions or requests concerning information under our control, please contact us using the details below.

Requests concerning information held by Google or other third-party providers may also need to be submitted directly to those providers.

You may be able to manage advertising preferences and privacy settings through your Android device and Google's available privacy controls.

For further information, visit: Google Privacy Information .
